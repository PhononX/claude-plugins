# What's new in the Chief of Staff

Shown after ./update-cos.sh, for each version newer than the one you had.

## 0.3.0
- Drafts: say "start a draft email to Dana about pricing" or "add this to the board narrative", and your agent shapes it with you, keeping every version. When it's ready, it asks whether to keep it or move it to your tool, like Gmail or Google Docs.
- Long-term pieces you build over time are never pruned: when one has gone quiet, your agent offers to pick it back up.
- Your voice: with your OK, your agent learns how you write for each kind of writing (work email, Slack, a text to a friend), and shows it in your notebook.
- A launcher: start your agent from Spotlight, Launchpad, the Dock or your Desktop.

## 0.2.0
- Your notebook page: everything your agent knows, on one page in your browser. Open it with ./open-brain.sh.
- Briefs: a morning brief in Carbon Voice, and more during the day if you like, with a catch-up on what needs your reply and numbered follow-ups ("do 1 and 3").
- Ask "what can you do?" to see everything you can turn on, and change any of it by saying so.
- Your rules and what your agent knows about you are kept in your notebook, where you can see and correct them.
